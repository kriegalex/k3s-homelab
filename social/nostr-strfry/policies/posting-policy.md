# Relay Posting Policy — nostr.21crypto.ch

This policy applies to the Nostr relay at `wss://nostr.21crypto.ch`, operated
by the admin pubkey listed in the relay's NIP-11 document.

## Jurisdiction

This relay is operated from **Switzerland**. Swiss law applies.

## Prohibited content

Anything that is illegal under Swiss law is not allowed. This includes, but is
not limited to:

- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind.
- Content inciting violence, hatred, or discrimination prohibited under Swiss
  criminal law (notably StGB Art. 261bis).
- Content that violates Swiss copyright, trademark, or other IP law.
- Content used to commit fraud, extortion, or other criminal offences.
- Malware, phishing, or content distributing exploits.

The operator may delete events and disconnect or block clients at sole
discretion, with or without notice, to comply with the law or to keep the
relay operable.

## No warranty

This relay is a **hobbyist homelab service**. It is provided **"as is"**, with
**no warranty** of availability, durability, performance, or fitness for any
purpose.

- **No backups are guaranteed.** Events may be lost at any time.
- **No uptime guarantees.** The relay may go offline without notice.
- **No data-recovery commitments.** Deleted or lost events will not be
  restored.

Do not rely on this relay as your only copy of anything you care about. Run
your own relay, or use multiple relays, if persistence matters to you.

## Privacy

See [privacy.md](privacy.md) for what is logged and how connection data is
handled.

Note that all events published to a Nostr relay are **public by design** and
are forwarded to any client that requests them.

## Contact

For policy questions, abuse reports, or legal notices, contact the admin via
Nostr DM to the pubkey listed in the relay's NIP-11 document.

## Changes

This policy may change without notice. The version in effect is the one
currently published in this repository.
